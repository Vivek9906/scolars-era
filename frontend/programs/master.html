Entry requirements

Academic

A recognised bachelor's degree or equivalent undergraduate qualification is required for postgraduate admission. Some programmes accept applicants with significant professional experience in lieu of a formal degree. We review every profile individually before advising on fit.

English Language

Applicants whose first language is not English will typically need to demonstrate proficiency through IELTS, TOEFL or an equivalent test. Minimum scores vary by institution and programme. We advise on the most appropriate route for your situation.
