Entry requirements

Academic

A completed secondary education or equivalent qualification is the standard requirement for undergraduate entry. Some programmes may ask for specific subject grades depending on the discipline. We review your academic background in full before making any recommendations.

English Language

Applicants whose first language is not English will typically need to demonstrate proficiency through IELTS, TOEFL or an equivalent test. Minimum scores vary by institution and programme. We advise on the most appropriate route for your situation.
