Course Overview

The Master of Education (M.Ed) is a postgraduate academic degree that prepares students for careers in education, including curriculum and instruction, counseling, school psychology, and administration. This advanced program is designed for educators who wish to enhance their teaching skills and leadership capabilities.

Duration

2 Years (4 Semesters)

Eligibility

B.Ed with minimum 50% marks from a recognized university.

Intake

July / January

Syllabus

  • Advanced Educational Psychology
  • Historical and Political Perspectives of Education
  • Educational Studies
  • Teacher Education 1 & 2
  • Curriculum Studies
  • Research Methods in Education

Admission Process

Admission to the M.Ed program is based on merit or entrance exams conducted by the respective universities. Candidates must submit their application forms along with required documents before the deadline.

Career Opportunities

Graduates with an M.Ed degree can explore various career paths such as:

  • School Principal / Administrator
  • Curriculum Developer
  • Educational Counselor
  • Education Policy Analyst
  • Lecturer / Professor in B.Ed Colleges
