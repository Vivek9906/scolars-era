Master of Education – Complete Course Details & Admission Guide

Course Overview

M.Ed (Master of Education) ek postgraduate program hai jo teaching, research aur educational leadership ke liye students ko prepare karta hai.

Duration

2 Years (4 Semesters)

Eligibility Criteria

  • B.Ed or equivalent degree
  • Minimum 50% marks (Relaxation as per norms)
  • Recognized University se degree honi chahiye

Syllabus

  • Philosophical & Sociological Foundations of Education
  • Educational Psychology
  • Research Methodology
  • Curriculum Studies
  • Teacher Education

Admission Process

Admission merit based ya entrance exam ke through hota hai. Kuch universities direct admission bhi deti hain.

Career Opportunities

  • Teacher / Lecturer
  • Education Consultant
  • Curriculum Developer
  • Academic Coordinator
  • Research Scholar / PhD
