Home / Courses / M.Sc

Course Overview

M.Sc (Master of Science) is a postgraduate degree in the field of science. It allows students to gain advanced knowledge in subjects like Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Computer Science, and more.

Duration

2 Years

Eligibility

B.Sc with min 50-55% aggregate marks.

Intake

June / July

Popular Specializations

  • M.Sc Physics
  • M.Sc Chemistry
  • M.Sc Mathematics
  • M.Sc Computer Science
  • M.Sc Biotechnology
  • M.Sc Data Science

Admission Process

Admission is often based on university-level entrance exams or national level exams like JAM (Joint Admission Test for Masters). Direct admission is also available in some colleges based on merit.

Career Opportunities

  • Research Scientist
  • Laboratory Manager
  • Data Analyst
  • Chemist / Biologist
  • Professor (after PhD)
