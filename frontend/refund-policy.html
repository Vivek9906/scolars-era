Refund Policy

This Refund Policy covers every enrolment made through www.scholarslift.com, operated by Scholars Lift LTD ("Scholars Lift," "the Company," "we," "us"). When you enrol in any program or Service through us, you confirm that you have read, understood, and accepted this Refund Policy along with our Terms and Conditions.

Because Scholars Lift facilitates enrolment in programs run by independent partner institutions, your refund eligibility depends on both our administrative policy below and the specific terms set by that institution. If a partner institution's refund terms are stricter than ours, those stricter terms apply to the portion of your fees passed on to them.

1. Cancelling Before the Program Starts

If you cancel before the official program start date (as stated in your enrolment confirmation or a follow-up email), you may qualify for a refund of the fees paid directly to Scholars Lift, minus any non-refundable administrative, counselling, or processing charges disclosed to you at enrolment.

Any portion of your fee already sent to a partner institution follows that institution's own refund and cancellation rules, which we will share with you before you enrol.

2. Trial or Cooling-Off Period

Some programs include a trial or cooling-off window, as noted in the program details at the time you enrol. If you withdraw during that window, you may be eligible for a refund minus applicable administrative and processing charges.

Once that window closes, no refund will be issued for that program, unless this Policy or the law says otherwise.

3. No Refunds Once a Program Has Started

Once a program officially begins (or once the trial window closes, whichever applies), fees generally become non-refundable, except where the partner institution's own policy allows for it.

We do not issue refunds for missed classes, partial attendance, unfinished coursework, or voluntary withdrawal after the program has started.

4. Honorary Doctorate Facilitation Fees

Fees for Honorary Doctorate facilitation cover Scholars Lift's administrative and coordination work, not the award itself. Once we have formally submitted your application to the partner institution, the facilitation fee becomes non-refundable, regardless of whether the institution ultimately confers, postpones, or declines the Honorary Doctorate.

If we have not yet submitted your application or documentation, you can request a cancellation and refund of the facilitation fee, minus any administrative charges already incurred, by contacting us as described in Section 7.

We are not responsible for, and will not refund fees based on, the partner institution's evaluation outcome, timeline, or any changes to its recognition or eligibility criteria.

5. When Refunds Do Not Apply

We do not provide refunds in these situations:

  • You change your mind after the cancellation or trial window has closed
  • You miss scheduled classes, counselling sessions, or assessments
  • Your access is suspended or terminated because you broke our Terms and Conditions
  • You are unable to finish the program for personal reasons unrelated to any fault of ours or the partner institution
  • Visa refusal, immigration delays, or similar issues outside our control, unless a specific visa-refusal refund clause was agreed to in writing when you enrolled

6. Duplicate or Mistaken Payments

If you made a duplicate payment or a payment was processed by mistake, let us know within 15 days of the transaction. Once we verify it, we will refund the amount to your original payment method within 7 to 14 working days.

7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

  • Email us at info@scholarslift.com with your enrolment ID, program name, and the reason for your request, or
  • Submit a request through your learner dashboard, if one is available to you

We will review your request against this Policy and the relevant partner institution's terms, and get back to you within roughly 10 business days.

8. How Long Refunds Take

Once approved, refunds are processed within 7 to 14 working days, sent to the original payment method used at enrolment, unless we agree on a different arrangement.

9. Financing, Loans, and Instalment Plans

If you financed your enrolment through a third-party loan or EMI provider, any refund we approve will first go toward settling your outstanding loan balance, based on your loan provider's terms. You remain responsible for covering any remaining shortfall.

If your loan has already been paid to us in full, your standard loan repayment obligations to your lender continue regardless of withdrawal, subject to the lender's own policies.

10. Corporate or Sponsored Enrolments

If an employer or sponsor paid your enrolment fee, any approved refund goes back to that paying party, not to you as the individual learner, unless we agree otherwise in writing.

11. Our Decisions Are Final

All refund decisions made under this Policy are final. At our sole discretion, we may make an exception in extenuating circumstances, but doing so once does not obligate us to do so again in the future.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy at any time. Changes take effect as soon as they are posted and apply to enrolments made after that date. Enrolments made before a change remain governed by whichever policy was in effect at the time, unless we state otherwise.
