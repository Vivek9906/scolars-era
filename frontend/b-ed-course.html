Bachelor of Education (B.Ed)

Home / Courses / B.Ed

Course Overview

Bachelor of Education (B.Ed) is an undergraduate professional degree that prepares students for work as a teacher in schools. It is mandatory for teaching at the secondary (classes 6-10) and higher secondary (classes 11-12) divisions.

Duration

2 Years (4 Semesters)

Eligibility

Graduation (Regular) with min 50% marks.

Intake

July / August

Syllabus / Subjects

  • Childhood and Growing Up
  • Contemporary India and Education
  • Learning and Teaching
  • Language across the Curriculum
  • Pedagogy of School Subjects
  • Assessment for Learning

Admission Process

Admissions are usually done through entrance exams conducted by various universities or states. Some colleges also offer merit-based candidates based on graduation marks.

Career Opportunities

  • School Teacher (Private/Govt)
  • Education Counsellor
  • Online Tutor
  • Content Writer (Education)
  • Vice Principal / Principal
