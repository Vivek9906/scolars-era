Frequently asked questions

How can Scholars Lift help with admissions?

We review your profile, suggest suitable programs, explain eligibility, and guide you through documentation and application steps.

Do you support PhD and research work?

Yes. We support research topic planning, synopsis, thesis, dissertation, manuscript preparation, and publication readiness.

Can working professionals apply?

Yes. Our counselling is suitable for working professionals who need flexible academic routes and guided documentation support.

How do I start?

Book a counselling session or send your query through the contact form. Our team will review your requirement and suggest the next steps.

What programs does Scholars Lift offer guidance for?

We offer guidance for a wide range of programs including B.Ed, B.Sc, M.Ed, M.Sc, M.Tech, PhD pathways, honorary doctorates, and professional certifications across multiple disciplines.

Is Scholars Lift a university?

No. Scholars Lift is an education consulting and academic facilitation platform. We partner with accredited universities and institutions to help learners find the right academic pathways.

How long does the admission process typically take?

The timeline varies depending on the program and university. On average, the process takes 2 6 weeks from profile review to final admission confirmation. Our team keeps you updated throughout.

Do you provide support for international students?

Absolutely. We work with learners across countries and time zones, offering personalised guidance for international admissions, documentation, and program selection.

What documents do I need for the application?

Typical requirements include academic transcripts, identity proof, passport-size photographs, a statement of purpose, and relevant work experience certificates. Our counsellors will provide a complete checklist based on your chosen program.

Is the initial counselling session free?

Yes. Your first counselling session is completely free. We use this session to understand your goals, evaluate your profile, and recommend the most suitable academic pathways for you.
