Master of Technology (M.Tech)

Home / Courses / M.Tech

Course Overview

M.Tech (Master of Technology) is a professional postgraduate engineering degree. It focuses on specialization in specific engineering disciplines, fostering advanced technical skills and research capabilities.

Duration

2 Years (4 Semesters)

Eligibility

B.Tech/B.E with valid GATE score or entrance.

Intake

July / August

Syllabus / Streams

  • Computer Science & Engineering
  • Civil Engineering (Structural/Geotech)
  • Mechanical Engineering (Thermal/Design)
  • Electronics & Communication
  • Data Science & AI
  • Research Methodology

Admission Process

Candidates generally need to clear the GATE (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering) exam. Some private universities conduct their own entrance tests or offer admission based on B.Tech merit.

Career Opportunities

  • Senior Engineer
  • Research Scientist (R&D)
  • Project Manager
  • Professor / Lecturer
  • Technical Consultant
