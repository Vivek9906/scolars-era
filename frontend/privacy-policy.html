Privacy Policy

Scholars Lift LTD ("Scholars Lift," "the Company," "we," "us," "our") runs www.scholarslift.com (the "Website") and provides academic and career consultancy services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and what choices you have. Please read it before using the Website. By continuing to use the Website, you agree to the practices described below.

1. What Information We Collect

Information you give us directly:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, nationality, and postal address
  • Academic records, qualifications, transcripts, and identity documents you submit for admission or counselling purposes
  • Billing and payment information, processed through our third-party payment gateway (we do not store your full card details)
  • Any messages you send us, including enquiry forms, live chat, and support tickets

Information collected automatically:

  • IP address, browser type, device details, and operating system
  • Pages you visit, time spent on each page, referral source, and click activity, gathered through cookies and similar tools

Information we receive from others:

  • Updates from partner institutions about your application or enrolment status
  • Data from advertising and analytics partners, such as Google Analytics and Meta Ads

2. Why We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide and improve our academic counselling and program facilitation services
  • Process your enrolment with partner institutions and keep you updated on your application status
  • Handle payments and send confirmations or invoices
  • Respond to enquiries and provide customer support
  • Send updates, offers, and marketing messages, which you can opt out of at any time
  • Analyse how the Website is used so we can make it better
  • Detect and prevent fraud, security issues, or technical problems
  • Meet our legal and regulatory obligations

3. Legal Basis for Processing (UK and EU Users)

Where data protection law requires it, we rely on one or more of the following grounds to process your personal data: your consent, the need to fulfil a contract with you (for example, enrolling you in a program), our legitimate business interests such as improving our Services, and our obligation to comply with the law.

4. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to run the Website, remember your preferences, and understand traffic patterns. These fall into four categories:

  • Essential cookies, needed for the Website to function
  • Performance and analytics cookies, which help us see how visitors use the Website
  • Functional cookies, which remember your settings
  • Advertising cookies, used by us or our partners to show relevant ads

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Keep in mind that turning off essential cookies may affect how the Website works.

5. Who We Share Your Information With

We may share your data with:

  • Partner institutions, to process your admission or enrolment application
  • Service providers, including payment processors, email and SMS providers, hosting companies, and analytics platforms, all bound by confidentiality obligations
  • Affiliates, meaning companies under common ownership with Scholars Lift LTD, who also follow this Policy
  • Legal and regulatory authorities, when required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights
  • A buyer or successor, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with equivalent privacy protections in place

We do not sell your personal data to third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

6. Where Your Data Is Processed

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including wherever our partner institutions or service providers are based. When this happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, in line with applicable data protection law.

7. How Long We Keep Your Data

We hold on to your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, including any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. After that, we securely delete or anonymise it.

8. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Delete your personal data, subject to certain exceptions
  • Restrict how we use your data
  • Object to our processing, including for direct marketing
  • Transfer your data to another organisation, where technically possible
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where our processing relies on it

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@scholarslift.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by law, usually within one month.

9. How We Protect Your Data

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. That said, no method of transmitting data over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Services are meant for people aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you think a minor has shared personal data with us, please let us know so we can take appropriate action.

11. Third-Party Links

Our Website may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so we encourage you to read their policies before sharing any information.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updated version will show a new "Effective Date" and will apply as soon as it is posted. We suggest checking back now and then.
